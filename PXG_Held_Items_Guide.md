# PokeXGames · Held Items Guide

## 1. What is a Held Item?
A Held Item is an item you equip on a Pokemon (or on your Device) to give it a bonus. Every Held Item has a Tier: the higher the Tier, the stronger and rarer it is. The Tier also sets its color: Tier 1 is brown and Tier 7 is red. A few items have their own color, such as Y-Antiburn.

- **Slot rule:** A Pokemon can carry at most two Held Items at once, and they must be of different types: one X and one Y.
- **X vs Y:** All Offensive and Defensive Holds are X. Utility Holds can be X or Y.
- **Main source:** The Duelist Brotherhood quest. You can also get them from Held Item Tickets and by fusing lower Tiers.

### Equipping and removing
- **Equip on a Pokemon:** Keep the Pokemon in your backpack, right-click the Held Item, left-click the Pokemon, wait 3 seconds, then press "Yes".
- **Equip on a Device:** Use the Held Item on the Device and confirm. The new Held replaces any previous one. The Improved Device has two modes (Default and Defensive), and the Held goes into the mode the Device is in at that moment.
- **Remove from a Pokemon:** Talk to NPC Apolo in the Trade Center (Pokemon outside its Pokeball) and pay the fee.
- **Remove from a Device:** NPC Atena, also in the Trade Center, for the same fee. On an Improved Device, she only removes the Held of the mode currently in use.

### Warnings
Once equipped on a Pokemon, a Held can only be removed by paying Apolo. Equipping a Held of the same type on a Pokemon that already has one (for example an X on an X) destroys the old one forever.

### X-Boost (stat bonus that depends on player level)
X-Boost gives HP equal to X levels of boost and double that amount as attack. It grows with the player's level range, except in PvP content. Its status-resistance bonus never goes above that of a +50 Pokemon.

## 3. Which Helds to use in PvP
*Slot rule reminder: one X and one Y per Pokemon. In PvP, many Y Helds do nothing, so the Y slot is mostly a situational choice.*

**Tier cap in PvP:** Held Items are capped at Tier 7 in PvP content. Tier 8 and Tier 9 Helds give no extra benefit there.

### Helds that do NOT help in PvP
- **Y-Cure:** Does not work in duels.
- **Y-Regeneration:** Only heals out of combat, does not work in duels.
- **Y-Control:** Does not work in PvP content.
- **Y-Teleport:** Only useful for Teleport in PvE.
- **X-Lucky & X-Experience:** Only affect drops and experience from wild Pokemon.
- **Y-Light, Y-Headbutt, Y-Dig, Y-Smash, Y-Cut:** Exploration skills, not for duels.
- **Y-Antiself & Y-Blur:** Obsolete, only used as fusion material.

### Best X Helds for PvP
| Held | Why it matters in PvP | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| X-Attack | Straight damage increase, up to +28% at Tier 7 | Damage |
| X-Critical | Adds critical hit chance, up to 24% at Tier 7 | Damage |
| X-Defense | Flat defense increase, up to +24% at Tier 7 | Tank / Bruiser |
| X-Block | Chance to cut 90% of a hit, up to 22% at Tier 7 | Tank |
| X-Vitality | More HP, up to +25% at Tier 7 | Tank |
| X-Accuracy | Helps status moves land | Disruptor |
| X-Poison / X-Hellfire | Big boosts to Poison/Burn damage (up to +300%) | Status Damage |
| X-Return | Reflects 2.5% to 9% of damage taken | Situational |
| X-Haste | Extra movement speed | Mobility |
| X-Boost | Gives HP and attack, scaling does not apply in PvP | Situational |
| X-Harden / Rage / Strafe / Agility | Random chance to use move while attacking | Situational |

### Best Y Helds for PvP
| Held | Why it matters in PvP | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| X-Elemental | Random passive chance. Reduced damage under level 100 | Situational |
| X-Cooldown | Shortens attack cooldowns (no effect on cooldowns 10 or less) | Situational |

### Example Setups
| Role | X Held | Y Held |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 4. Getting better Helds: fusion and tickets
### Fusion machine
- Located on New Island. Requires completing the Mewtwo Clones quest at least once.
- Put in **3 Helds of the same Tier** (they do not need to be the same Held) and pay the fee to get one random Held of a higher Tier.
- **X-Block cannot be fused.**
- Fusing three Tier 7 Helds only gives: Defense, Wing, Teleport, Lucky, Critical or Attack.

| Fusion | T1>T2 | T2>T3 | T3>T4 | T4>T5 | T5>T6 | T6>T7 | T7>T7 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fee | 60,000 | 150,000 | 300,000 | 700,000 | 1,000,000 | 1,500,000 | 1,500,000 |

### Held Item Tickets
- Give 1 Ticket to NPC Victoria to get 1 X Held: X-Attack, X-Defense, X-Critical, X-Block or X-Accuracy.
- Tickets come in Tiers 3 to 7. Tier 6 and 7 can be earned in the Global Tournament, and some Tier 4, 5 and 7 Tickets come from quests.
