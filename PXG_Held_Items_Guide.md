## PokéXGames Held Items Guide

How the Held system works, and which Held Items to use in PvP

How to read this guide: facts come from the official PXG wiki. Points marked [Author note] come from the guide author's own PvP experience and are not stated on the wiki. Points marked [Analysis] are conclusions drawn from the wiki's effect descriptions, not an official meta. Game values can change after patches, so re-check the wiki before spending resources.

## 1. What is a Held Item?

A Held Item is an item you equip on a Pokémon (or on your Device) to give it a bonus. Every Held Item has a Tier: the higher the Tier, the stronger and rarer it is. The Tier also sets its color: Tier 1 is brown and Tier 7 is red. A few items have their own color, such as Y-Antiburn.

- Slot rule: a Pokémon can carry at most two Held Items at once, and they must be of different types: one X and one Y.

- X vs Y: all Offensive and Defensive Helds are X. Utility Helds can be X or Y.

- Main source: the Duelist Brotherhood quest. You can also get them from Held Item Tickets and by fusing lower Tiers (section 4).

## Equipping and removing

- Equip on a Pokémon: keep the Pokémon in your backpack, right-click the Held Item, left-click the Pokémon, wait 3 seconds, then press "Yes".

- Equip on a Device: use the Held Item on the Device and confirm. The new Held replaces any previous one. The Improved Device has two modes (Default and Defensive), and the Held goes into the mode the Device is in at that moment, so check it first.

- Remove from a Pokémon: talk to NPC Apolo in the Trade Center (Pokémon outside its Pokéball) and pay the fee.

- Remove from a Device: NPC Atena, also in the Trade Center, for the same fee. On an Improved Device she only removes the Held of the mode currently in use.

## Warnings

- Once equipped on a Pokémon, a Held can only be removed by paying Apolo.

- Equipping a Held of the same type on a Pokémon that already has one (for example an X on an X) destroys the old one forever. Example from the wiki: using an X-Lucky on a Pokémon wearing an X-Elemental deletes the X-Elemental.

## X-Boost (stat bonus that depends on player level)

X-Boost gives HP equal to X levels of boost and double that amount as attack. It grows with the player's level range, except in PvP content. Its status-resistance bonus never goes above that of a +50 Pokémon.

## 3. Which Helds to use in PvP

Remember the slot rule: one X and one Y per Pokémon. In PvP, many Y Helds do nothing, so the Y slot is mostly a situational choice.

## Tier cap in PvP

- [Author note] Held Items are capped at Tier 7 in PvP content. Tier 8 and Tier 9 Helds give no extra benefit there, so all PvP values in this section are shown at Tier 7.

- [Analysis] For PvP builds, spending on Tier 8 upgrades (X-Upgrade, Y-Teleport Upgrade, Y-Wing Upgrade) will not improve your PvP performance. Tier 7 is the target.

## Helds that do NOT help in PvP

- Y-Cure: does not work in duels (wiki). Do not count on it to clear status in PvP.

- Y-Regeneration: only heals out of combat, and the wiki says it does not work in duels either.

- Y-Control: [Author note] does not work in PvP content, so skip it even on Pokémon with Control Mind.

- Y-Teleport: [Author note] only useful for Teleport in PvE. It just shortens the Teleport cooldown and adds nothing in a fight.

- X-Lucky and X-Experience: [Analysis] they only affect drops and experience from wild Pokémon.

- Y-Light, Y-Headbutt, Y-Dig, Y-Smash, Y-Cut: [Analysis] exploration skills, not meant for duels.

- Y-Antiself and Y-Blur: obsolete, only used as fusion material.

## PvP tips: X-Defense vs X-Block [Author note]

- For long tankiness, pick X-Defense over X-Block. If the goal is to survive for a long time and stay alive until your cooldowns refresh, X-Defense is better. It lowers every hit you take, while X-Block only works on a random chance.

- X-Block is great for Healers and support Pokémon. This applies to Healers that do not need X-Accuracy to disturb the enemy and whose only goal is to give support or heal. They are weak and die easily, so X-Block can shield them better than X-Defense.

## Important detail

- The wiki says Y-Cure and Y-Regeneration do not work in duels. This guide treats that as meaning PvP duels. If you notice a different behavior in another PvP mode, trust what you see in game and report it.

## 4. Getting better Helds: fusion and tickets

## Fusion machine

- The machine is on New Island. You must have completed the Mewtwo Clones quest at least once.

- Put in 3 Helds of the same Tier (they do not need to be the same Held) and pay the fee. You receive one random Held of a higher Tier.

- X-Block cannot be fused.

- Fusing three Tier 7 Helds only gives: Defense, Wing, Teleport, Lucky, Critical or Attack.

| Fusion | T1>T2 | T2>T3 | T3>T4 | T4>T5 | T5>T6 | T6>T7 | T7>T7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fee | 60,000 | 150,000 | 300,000 | 700,000 | 1,000,000 | 1,500,000 | 1,500,000 |

Fees are in dollars (in-game currency).

## Held Item Tickets

- Give 1 Ticket to NPC Victoria to get 1 X Held: X-Attack, X-Defense, X-Critical, X-Block or X-Accuracy.

- Tickets come in Tiers 3 to 7. Tier 6 and 7 can be earned in the Global Tournament, and some Tier 4, 5 and 7 Tickets come from quests (such as Nena, Borin and Mysteries from the Ruins of Alph).
